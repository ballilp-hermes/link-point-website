How we work

Four steps, each with a document at the end of it.

  1. Enquiry and specification

    You tell us the duty point, the voltage and the market. We confirm which model fits and what certification your market requires before anything is priced.

  2. Quotation

    A written quotation carrying the model, the FOB port, the packing data and the validity period — so your landed cost can be calculated, not guessed.

  3. Sample

    A sample unit for evaluation. We ask for feedback on performance, packing and price expectation; that feedback shapes the production order.

  4. Production and shipping

    Pre-shipment inspection, then documents: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin.

Quality and compliance

We would rather send you a certificate number than an adjective. Any specification, certification or efficiency claim we make is tied to a document you can check.

  • Certification stated per model, with certificate number and test report on request.
  • Customs classification confirmed for each product family before a quotation is issued.
  • Pre-shipment inspection against a written checklist, with photographs.
  • Spare-parts list issued with the first order, not after the first failure.
  • Warranty terms stated in the quotation — manufacturing defects are the factory's responsibility, first-line response is ours.

Where a certification is not yet held for a model, we say so. We do not advertise approvals we cannot produce.
