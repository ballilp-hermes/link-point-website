What the cluster gives you

Deep supply of castings, motors, seals and controllers within a few kilometres; a labour force that has built pumps for two generations; and cost structures that a European or Turkish factory cannot match.

What the cluster does not give you

Consistency. With 3,700 manufacturers, quality ranges from export-grade to domestic-only, and the difference is rarely visible in a photograph or a quotation. Buying direct from a factory you have never audited is a lottery with a shipping bill attached.

Where Link Point sits

We are the party a buyer can hold responsible outside China. We select the model against the duty point, confirm the customs classification, inspect before shipment, and coordinate warranty and spare parts after it lands.

How we work

Four steps, each with a document at the end of it.

  1. Enquiry and specification

    You tell us the duty point, the voltage and the market. We confirm which model fits and what certification your market requires before anything is priced.

  2. Quotation

    A written quotation carrying the model, the FOB port, the packing data and the validity period — so your landed cost can be calculated, not guessed.

  3. Sample

    A sample unit for evaluation. We ask for feedback on performance, packing and price expectation; that feedback shapes the production order.

  4. Production and shipping

    Pre-shipment inspection, then documents: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin.

Where the engineering comes from

Most pump exporters buy a finished unit and print a catalogue. The motor and drive inside ours began as research, not as a purchased design.

7granted patents in motor and drive technology, held by the group's engineering company
6of those began as Tsinghua University intellectual property, contributed into the company as equity
200+research collaborations behind the engineering team, including 30+ National Natural Science Foundation projects

Motor control, permanent-magnet and hybrid-excitation machines, and the synchronous-reluctance drive granted in 2023 come out of a joint research centre with the Department of Electrical Engineering at Tsinghua University. That is the technical reason a Wenling pump can hold pressure at low flow and survive a pond in August — not a marketing line about "advanced technology".

Tsinghua University does not manufacture, certify or endorse these products. The relationship is a research and intellectual-property one. Every performance, efficiency and noise figure we publish is evidenced by our own test data, available on request.

Questions buyers ask before the first order

Where are Link Point's pumps made?

In Wenling, Zhejiang — the largest pump and motor cluster in China, which produces over 70% of the country's small pumps. Our manufacturer is Tsingling Drive (清岭智驱), whose drive electronics come from the group's own SMT plant in the same district.

Are you a manufacturer or a trading company?

We are the exporter and distributor, based in Hong Kong. We do not manufacture. That matters: a factory sells what it makes, we sell what your market will reorder — and we will tell you when a model does not suit your application.

Which markets do you supply?

First phase: the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Indonesia and Vietnam, with Thailand, the Philippines and India next. The European Union, United States and Australia follow once the certification for each model family is in place.

What is the minimum order quantity?

It depends on the model — pumps and aerators carry different minimums, and mixed-model orders are possible subject to the factory schedule. The MOQ is stated on every quotation rather than in a general policy, because it changes with the size and voltage of the unit.

Can you supply 110V/60Hz pumps and aerators?

Yes, on request. 60Hz variants are needed for the Philippines and parts of the Americas; the tooling exists, and the voltage and frequency are confirmed per model before the order is placed.

What are your shipping terms and documents?

FOB Ningbo or Shanghai by default, CIF on request. Every shipment is documented with a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin, and the goods are declared under the correct customs heading for the product family.

Do you offer OEM and private label?

Yes — your branding on the product, the carton and the manual, with plug, cable and manual localised for the destination market. Tooling changes and minimum quantities are quoted per model.
